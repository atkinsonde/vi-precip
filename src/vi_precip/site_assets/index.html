Data

  • Daily precipitation and temperature: Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC), MSC GeoMet OGC API, climate-daily and climate-stations collections (api.weather.gc.ca).
  • Homogenised monthly data (cross-check): ECCC Adjusted and Homogenized Canadian Climate Data (AHCCD), ahccd-monthly.
  • River flow and water level: Water Survey of Canada. Approved daily means (HYDAT) from the Water Office daily_data service; provisional daily means from real_time_data. Provisional data can change.
  • Climate indices: NOAA PSL (Niño 3.4, Niño 3, Niño 4, ONI, SOI, PNA, PDO, MEI.v2), NOAA CPC relative Niño 3.4 (RONI).

Precipitation

  • Target period: September 1 to the end date; the same calendar dates are used in every year. Reference period = 2000–2025; "typical" means the reference-period median for the same calendar dates (not ECCC's official 1991–2020 climate normals). The target year is never part of its own reference.
  • A period total counts if no more than 2 days are missing. Multi-day accumulations (flag A, and the "C" days that precede them) count fully in totals but not in day-by-day measures. Trace (T) = 0 mm.
  • Stations: Tier 1 ≥ 22 valid reference years, Tier 2 ≥ 12, both with a valid target period. Stations whose ID changed at the same site are joined into composites only after an overlap check.
  • Wet day ≥ 1.0 mm (primary) or ≥ 0.2 mm (sensitivity). Accumulation blocks above the threshold are split with an unbiased wet-day estimator.
  • Ranks are among the reference years plus the target year ("3rd driest of 27" = only two drier years since 2000). Return periods: empirical, and from a gamma fit with a mixed zero (bootstrap interval).
  • Driest N-day period: the lowest running N-day total that starts on or after Sep 1 and ends by the end date.

Temperature

  • Period means of daily max, min and range. A period counts with ≤ 5 missing days per 30 and ≤ 3 in a row. Only composites without a temperature step are joined.

Rivers and lakes

  • Mean flow (or level for lakes) over the period, ranked against all years with ≤ 2 missing days since the stated year.
  • Regulation is assigned from a reviewed list; unlisted sites are marked "not checked".
  • ENSO analysis uses only natural rivers: Reference Hydrometric Basin Network gauges plus reviewed unregulated gauges.

ENSO correspondence

  • Every station series is converted to rank-based standard scores (z-scores from ranks); regional series are the mean score, re-scored.
  • Primary test, fixed in advance: Vancouver Island Sep 1–end precipitation against Jul–Sep Niño 3.4, one-sided (El Niño → drier), linear trends removed, Spearman correlation, permutation p-value. Repeated on the long record (1950 onward) as a check.
  • All other combinations (seasons × indices × measures × records) are exploratory and controlled with Benjamini–Hochberg FDR. Station maps use the Wilks (2016) field-significance rule.
  • Composites: El Niño / neutral / La Niña years by ONI JAS ± 0.5 °C.
  • A correlation is not a forecast: with about 26 years even a real ENSO effect of modest size may not reach significance, and a significant one explains only part of any single year.
